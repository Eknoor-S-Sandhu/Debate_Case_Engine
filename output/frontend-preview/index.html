Public transport should be free.

Net Benefits   /   Poverty + environment

01   Fare savings protect basic consumption

Uniqueness

Necessary journeys compete with essential spending for riders whose budgets are already stretched.

Link → Internal link

Removing the fare leaves money available for the journeys people already make. Where households have unmet needs, those savings can help pay for food and utilities.

Terminal impact

The intended benefit is fewer skipped meals and fewer periods without essential services.

Evidence gap: the affected population and net household savings remain unresolved.
